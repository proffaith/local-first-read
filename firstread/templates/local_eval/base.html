Student text never leaves this machine. The only outbound traffic is citation identifiers: for cases and statutes, the volume, reporter, page, party names and year (CourtListener, Cornell LII); for academic sources, a DOI or similar identifier, or the source's title and year (Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, doi.org). Author names, the sentence a source is cited for, and the rest of the paper are never sent. Everything here is preliminary — a triage draft, not a grade.
