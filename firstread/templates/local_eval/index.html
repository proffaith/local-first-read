Each run gets a subfolder here with the reports, the scrubbed text the model saw, and the audit log. Reports quote student text, so keep them where your institution keeps student records (for example a folder in your college's OneDrive or SharePoint). Remembered for next time.

What leaves this machine

  • Nothing from the paper itself. Text is scrubbed by the PII gate and only ever sent to the Ollama model on localhost.
  • Citation identifiers only. For cases and statutes, the volume, reporter, page, party names and year (e.g. 123 F.3d 456) go to CourtListener and Cornell LII so the authority can be found and its opinion fetched. For academic sources, a DOI or similar identifier, or the source's title and year, go to Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex and doi.org. Author names, the sentence a source is cited for, and the rest of the paper are never sent; personal communications, course materials and web links are never looked up.
  • A local audit line per file is appended to _emissions.jsonl in the run folder, listing exactly what was sent where.
